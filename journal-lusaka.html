Two Months Coaching Football in Lusaka

Lusaka, Zambia

Two months coaching football in Lusaka is what actually changed how I see travel. Victoria Falls was staggering. The safaris were the kind of thing you don't quite believe even while you're in them. But the part that stayed with me longest was smaller than that — the five-a-side games on dirt pitches after school, the walk to the market, conversations with people who had no reason to explain their day-to-day life to a stranger from England, but did anyway. That's where I actually started paying attention.

I'm not claiming any of that as hardship, and I'm well aware of how fortunate I am to have been able to go at all. What it gave me instead was a genuine appetite — for the places that don't make it onto a list of things to see, and for the version of a country that exists whether or not anyone's photographing it.

This is the trip that started everything on this site, if I'm honest — the reason "Places" exists alongside "Journal," and the reason the gallery isn't just landmarks. More from this trip is coming as I go back through the photos properly.
